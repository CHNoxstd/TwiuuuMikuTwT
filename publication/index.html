Ocean, climate & coastal futures

Four connected chapters synthesize selected research on a changing ocean, coastal ecosystems, climate risk, and adaptation. The series uses an assessment-style structure to make evidence, context, uncertainty, and choices easier to examine.

Scope and attribution. This is an independent educational resource, not an IPCC publication, assessment, or endorsed product. It does not reproduce IPCC report text. The references below point readers to the underlying literature and official assessments.

How to use this synthesis

Each chapter distinguishes established physical or ecological processes from context-dependent outcomes and proposed responses. A cited source supports further reading; it does not mean every statement in this learning resource has been independently peer reviewed.

Coastal conditions vary with geomorphology, ecology, governance, and lived experience. The chapters are a starting point for inquiry—not a local forecast, site assessment, engineering design, or substitute for community-led knowledge.
