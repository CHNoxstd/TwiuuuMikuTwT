measurement leave out?

A QUICK READING FRAME

Three layers of a good note.

OBSERVATION

What was directly seen?

Use a clear description. Keep measured values and direct observations separate from interpretations.

CONTEXT

Under what conditions?

Record time, method, tide, weather, visibility, and other factors that may affect comparison.

UNCERTAINTY

What is still unknown?

State gaps honestly. A well-described limitation is more useful than an unsupported confident conclusion.
